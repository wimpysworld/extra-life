Retro gaming appliance

Extra Life

Extra Life is a clean, uncluttered console operating system that gives extra life to classic games and hardware. Turn on your handheld, Raspberry Pi, or personal computer (PC) and start playing immediately.

The system boots straight into your game library with zero text consoles, zero emulator setup menus, and automatic updates.

Features

Built like a console

Extra Life turns your device into a dedicated game console that boots straight into the Ludo game menu.

01 Pure Console Experience
Turn on your device and browse your library with a controller. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth controllers, and audio settings live in one simple menu.
02 Ready to Play Out of the Box
Play over 40 classic consoles, handhelds, and arcade systems right away. You do not need to download emulator cores or tune complex settings.
03 Install Once, Updates Itself
Download over-the-air (OTA) updates straight from the settings menu. Every update keeps an automatic backup of the previous version for safe recovery.
04 Easy Wi-Fi and SD Card Transfers
Copy game files onto the SD card, or turn on File Sharing over Wi-Fi. Open the shared folder in Windows File Explorer, macOS Finder, or your Linux file manager.

Easy game transfers

Add your games in minutes

You do not need command-line tools to load your library. Copy your games over Wi-Fi or plug your storage card into your computer.

Option 01

Wi-Fi Network Drive (Windows, macOS, and Linux)

Turn on Samba File Sharing in the settings menu. Open the shared folder on your computer and drag your games into the roms folder.

Windows File Explorer
\\extra-life\Retro
macOS Finder & Linux File Managers
smb://extra-life.local/Retro
Option 02

SD Card or USB Drive

Plug your SD card or USB drive into your computer. Open the STORAGE drive and copy your games into the matching system folder inside Retro/roms.

Game Folders
STORAGE/Retro/roms/<System Name>/
System BIOS Files
STORAGE/Retro/system/

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the right download for my device?

Open the Device Finder and type your device model or choose your brand. Every device card shows the exact Extra Life image name and download link for your hardware.

How do I copy games from Windows, macOS, or Linux?

You can copy games directly onto the STORAGE partition of your SD card or USB drive inside Retro/roms. You can also turn on Samba File Sharing in the settings menu and connect to \\extra-life\Retro on Windows or smb://extra-life.local/Retro on macOS and Linux.

How do automatic updates work?

Open the settings menu on your device and select Check for Updates. Extra Life downloads the new system image in the background and installs it when you restart. The system keeps your previous version as a backup so that you can recover safely if an update fails.

Where do BIOS files go?

Some disc-based systems, such as PlayStation, Sega Saturn, and PC Engine CD, need Basic Input/Output System (BIOS) files. Copy your BIOS files into the Retro/system folder on your SD card or over the Wi-Fi network drive.
